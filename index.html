A bureau for the things we could build next.

Portland Futures Bureau is a civic laboratory focused on expanding what Portland imagines is possible. It exists to explore, prototype, discuss, and surface new ideas for improving life in Portland through public finance, institutional design, social infrastructure, civic experimentation, and practical problem solving.

We are grounded in action and experimentation. We value practical examples over abstract theories. We are interested in builders, solvers, operators, organizers, public servants, volunteers, and anyone creating tangible outcomes in their communities.

Instead of waiting for the perfect pilot, what if we staged several and saw what works? Fail or succeed, we aggregate what we learned and share it publicly.
